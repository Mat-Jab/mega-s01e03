The three problems

parser — the shared parser reads day-first dates month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first books 7 April and the invite says 4 July. Any channel, every tenant on file. Fixed.

hint — booking-v3 tells people to type month first, then reads the date day first. The date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what it says; the form reads the tenant’s way. Only reports through the form, only tenants on booking-v3. It looks like the parser bug (a flipped month), so subject matching put it in #4471. The parser fix does not touch it. The form is retired, so no new reports can come this way.

One-day move — bookings shift by a day. These reports flip nothing: Tuesday 12 May becomes Monday 11 May, a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto get different days for one meeting, an invite arrives before its booking, a month where every booking is one day off. No day/month reading produces that, so the parser fix cannot close it. It points at time zones (conversion to UTC, or a DST boundary). Still open.

For the desk

  • Matching on subject merged three bugs into one known ticket (decision 0001). Before you merge into a big ticket, read what the report shows.
  • A report that does not fit the ticket’s bug gets a ticket of its own, not a footnote.
  • Closing a merged ticket closes only the bugs its fixes touch.
